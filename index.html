# Privacy Policy for Mini PT **Last updated:** November 09, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how Mini PT ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Mini PT mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. ## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It Mini PT is designed as a local-first media utility. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or sell your personal data. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. ### A. Local Device Data (Not Uploaded) * **Media Files:** With your permission, the App accesses your device's local audio and video files (via MediaStore) to display your library, play media, and apply effects. This data never leaves your device. * **App Data:** Your bookmarks, watch history, playlists, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Room databases and SharedPreferences. * **Account Information:** If you use the Auto-Login feature, your email and password are encrypted and stored locally on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences. We do not have access to your credentials. * **Vault Files:** Files you move into the App's Vault are stored in the App's private local storage directory. They are deleted if you uninstall the App. ### B. Web Browsing Data The App includes a WebView browser (Web Hub and Stream Engine). When you use these features, standard web browsing data (such as cookies, cache, and local storage) is processed by the websites you visit (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, and curated streaming sites). You can clear this data at any time via the App's settings menu. ### C. Third-Party Services and Network Requests The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you: * **Advertising Networks:** We use Google AdMob and its mediation partners (including Pangle) to serve ads that keep Mini PT free. These networks may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general location to serve personalized ads. You can manage your ad personalization settings via your Android device settings and the consent prompts within the App. * **Cover Art & Metadata Lookup:** To fetch album art and artist names for local music files, the App sends track title and artist name strings over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to third-party databases (Deezer API and MusicBrainz/Cover Art Archive). No personal user data is transmitted during these lookups. * **Curated Feeds:** The App fetches a JSON file from a public GitHub repository to update the curated list of legal streaming sites in the Stream Engine. ## 2. Permissions We Request and Why The App requests the following Android permissions to function correctly: * **Read Media Audio / Read Media Video / Storage:** Required to scan your device for local music and videos to display in the App. * **Internet:** Required to load web pages in the Web Hub, show ads, and fetch album art. * **Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW):** Required for the Floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP) video player feature. * **Foreground Service:** Required to keep music and video playback active in the background or floating window. ## 3. Data Retention and Deletion Because all personal data is stored locally on your device, you are in complete control of it. You can delete your data at any time by: * Using the "Clear browsing data" option in the App's menu. * Unhiding or deleting files from the Vault. * Clearing the App's cache or data in your Android System Settings. * Uninstalling the App (this will permanently delete all local databases, vault files, and preferences). ## 4. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions. ## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. ## 6.
